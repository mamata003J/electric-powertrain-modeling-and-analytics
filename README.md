## Project Overview

This university team project focuses on designing and developing a
digital twin of an electric vehicle (EV) powertrain for the Electric
Mobility showroom.

The project involves power system modeling, data analytics, simulation,
and visualization. The work begins with understanding the relevant
powertrain components and continues toward the preparation of a pilot
digital twin.

## Project Objectives

The main objectives of the project are to:

- Understand the main components and operation of an EV powertrain
- Develop suitable powertrain models
- Perform energy and data analysis
- Carry out simulations of the powertrain system
- Visualize important data and simulation results
- Develop a digital twin concept for the EV powertrain
- Prepare a pilot demonstration

## Main Project Areas

### Powertrain Modeling
Study the EV powertrain and develop suitable models for the project.

### Energy and Data Analytics
Analyze relevant power, energy, and system data.

### Simulation
Use the developed models to study the behavior of the EV powertrain.

### Visualization
Present important data and simulation results using suitable
visualization methods.

### Digital Twin and Pilot
Combine the models, data analysis, simulation, and visualization toward
a digital twin and pilot demonstration.
